`useGlobalClickOutside` is removed, and `useClickOutside` now takes an options object: `useClickOutside({ onClickOutside, enabled, ignore })`, with `ignore` an explicit list of refs and selectors. It no longer ignores dialogs, menus and portals by itself, so list the ones your element opens, and it listens only while `enabled`. The `@msqd/lydia/Components/Utilities/useGlobalClickOutside` subpath is gone. `TransactionsListPage` no longer clears its selection on a click outside, and `onTransactionSelect` is never called with `null`. Pass `selectedId` (`null` for none) and clear it where you close the drawer that shows the transaction, from its `onClose`; without `selectedId` the page keeps its own selection, which only another row click changes.
