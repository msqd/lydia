`SummarySection` and `OverviewPage` no longer link to `/transactions` by default, nor print "24h" in the pane titles and average labels. Pass `summary.links` (`{ performance?, transactions?, errors? }`, one target per "details" link; a pane without target renders none), `summary.linkComponent` (a `LinkComponentType`, to go through your router) and `summary.windowLabel` (e.g. `"24h"`, shown as the titles' "(24h)" suffix and the "24h average" label) to get them back.
