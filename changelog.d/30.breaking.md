`NavBar` draws only what it is given. `navigation` and `userNavigation` default to `[]` (no demo links, no "Your Profile / Settings / Sign out" menu), `logo` defaults to nothing and `brand` to the text "lydia". `DefaultLogo` and `LydiaLogotype` are removed, and a string `logo` is no longer accepted: pass a `ReactNode`. Pass `navigation`, `userNavigation`, `logo` and `brand` to keep a branded bar. `TransactionsDetailPage` loses `isDrawer`, `onBack`, its built-in `DefaultLayout` and its "Transaction details" title: it renders its sections only, so wrap it in `DefaultLayout` (and its `title`) when it is a page.
