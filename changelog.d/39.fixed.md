The explorer tree counts and lists the right transactions: transactions without an `apiName` appear under the `unknown-api` node with their count, a query string or host no longer becomes a tree node, and `/api/user` no longer counts `/api/users/…`. `TransactionsExplorerSidebar` expands and collapses on its own when `expandedNodes` is not given, and `onToggleExpand` alone reports the changes.
