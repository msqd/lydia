`DataTable` keys rows by `rowKey` (default `id`), so a stateful cell stays with its row across pages, sorts and filters instead of its position. The transaction date filter and `DateRangeSelector`'s `disablePastDates` / `disableFutureDates` use local calendar days in every time zone, and an invalid date is no bound. New `parseLocalDay` and `localToday` helpers in `Models/Filters`.
