`TransactionsExplorerSidebar` reports a structured `Selection` (`{ type, apiName, segments }`) through `onSelect` instead of a path string and a type string, and its `selectedPath` prop becomes `selected?: Selection | null`. `TreeNode` gains `apiName` and `segments`. `Selection`, `apiOf`, `segmentsOf` and `isUnder` are exported to build and filter on the same endpoint model.
